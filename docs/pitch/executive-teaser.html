The 30-second version

Assume the API is public. Make abuse non-actionable.

Static analysis of any banking APK exposes every endpoint, schema, and embedded key — this cannot be fixed by hiding. We don't try to. We move trust to your server: the app proves its integrity, receives only short-lived request-bound credentials, and your backend rejects everything else. A captured request cannot be replayed or forged at scale.

What we deliberately do NOT sell
"Invisible" or "un-reverse-engineerable" APIs. That claim is false on mobile and fails a bank security review. OWASP: it is impossible to prevent static analysis.
What we sell — and can defend
A leaked endpoint or key is useless to anything but your genuine, unmodified app. Same architecture banks already accept under PSD2 & PCI. Candor passes due diligence.
